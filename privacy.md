# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Hermes Personal ("the app") is a private assistant built and used by a single person, Serhiy Pemakhov ("the owner"). The app is not offered to the public.

## What data the app accesses

Once the owner grants explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app may access the following data in the owner's Google account:

- Gmail: reading, labeling, moving messages to trash, and sending emails;
- Google Calendar: reading and managing events;
- Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: reading and editing files;
- Google Contacts: read-only access.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests, such as summarizing new emails, cleaning up the inbox, or managing the calendar. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training general-purpose AI models.

## Storage and sharing
  
- OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
- Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The only exception is processing by the AI model provider the owner uses to carry out a requested task.
- The app does not keep copies of emails or files beyond what a task requires.

## Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.
  
## Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
